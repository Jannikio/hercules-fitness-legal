Account deletion

To request deletion of your Hercules Fitness account and associated synced data, email us from the email address used for your account.

Request deletion by email

What will be deleted

We delete your app account, synced workout history, custom exercises, workout templates, app settings, and related sync records from production systems.

Local-only data

Data stored only on your device can be removed by deleting the app or clearing the app data from your device settings.

Processing time

We process deletion requests within 30 days. Limited records may be retained only where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup-retention reasons.

Include “Hercules Fitness account deletion” in your message so we can identify and process the request correctly.
