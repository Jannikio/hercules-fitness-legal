1. Delete your account

You can delete your Hercules account and everything synced with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) next to your account and choose Delete Account.
  3. Confirm.

Your account and all your synced data are deleted from our servers right away, and the synced data is removed from that phone.

Without the app

Email us from the email address of your account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Request deletion by email

If you signed in with Apple and chose “Hide My Email”, tell us the relay address Apple created for Hercules. You find it in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

What is deleted

Your account (user ID, email address and name) and all synced data: workouts, sets, exercise order, templates, custom exercises and settings.

Data that is only on your phone

Without an account, your data exists only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Backups

Copies in our service providers' backups are deleted when those backups expire. We never restore a deleted account from them.

3. Using Hercules without an account

Every feature of Hercules works without an account. Your workouts, templates, custom exercises and settings are then stored only in a database on your phone, in storage that only the app can access. We don't receive any of it, and you don't give us any personal details.

Storing this data on your phone is technically necessary for the app to do what you ask of it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is required. The data stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

Hercules asks for no device permissions besides internet access, which only sign-in and sync use. It doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

4. Signing in and syncing

Signing in is optional. It backs up your data on our servers and keeps it in sync across your devices.

Sign-in

You can sign in with Google or with Apple. Through our sign-in service (Supabase Auth) we then receive a user ID, your email address and, if the provider shares it, your name. With Google we also receive a link to your profile picture. With Apple you can hide your email address; we then receive a relay address instead. Google and Apple process data for the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies (see section 7).

Synced data

While you are signed in, the app syncs your training data:

  • workouts: start and end time, notes and the template used
  • sets: weight, reps, RPE and their order
  • the order of the exercises in each workout
  • templates: name, description and exercises with target sets, reps and notes
  • custom exercises: name, muscle group and equipment
  • settings: weight unit and theme

Each entry includes the time it was created and last changed. Data you created before signing in is added to your account when you sign in. Hercules doesn't record body measurements, health conditions or other medical information.

Your profile picture

If you sign in with Google, Settings shows your Google profile picture, which the app loads from Google's servers. Google receives your IP address when the picture loads.

Legal basis

We process this data to provide the account and sync you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Signing out stops syncing on that phone; your data stays on the phone.

5. Technical data

When the app connects to our servers, Supabase and PowerSync process the technical data every such connection needs: your IP address, the time of each request, sign-in tokens and details of the app's requests. They use it to provide sign-in and sync and to protect the service against misuse, and keep it in their logs for a short time.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the service securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to answer you. We keep them until your request is handled, and a short note that a deletion or privacy request was handled for as long as we need to show it was. Our support mailbox is a Gmail account, so Google stores the emails you send us.

Legal basis: for requests about your account, providing our service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for requests about your rights, our legal obligation to handle them (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Service providers

We use these services to run Hercules:

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore) hosts our database and sign-in service. Your synced data is stored in the EU. Supabase processes it only on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. Privacy policy
  • PowerSync (Journey Mobile, Inc.) runs the sync service that keeps the database on your phone and our database in step. It stores synced data in its US region and processes it only on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. Privacy policy
  • Google provides Sign in with Google, Google Play and our support mailbox (Gmail), and processes data for these as an independent controller. Privacy policy
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and the App Store, and processes data for these as an independent controller. Privacy policy
  • GitHub hosts this web page (see section 14).

When you download Hercules, Google Play or the App Store processes your download under its own terms. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We disclose it to others only where the law requires us to.

8. Transfers outside the EU

Synced data is stored in the EU by Supabase and in the US by PowerSync. Supabase is based in Singapore and may access data stored in the EU from there, for example for support. For these transfers, both providers have agreed to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Google and Apple may process sign-in data in the US; their privacy policies describe the safeguards they use. Countries outside the EU may not protect data to the same level; for example, US authorities may be able to access data stored in the US.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Your account and synced data: as long as your account exists. Deleting the account deletes them right away (see section 1).
  • Entries you delete in the app: hidden on all your devices. On our servers the record stays, marked as deleted, so your other devices learn about the deletion; it is erased when you delete your account. Email us if you want single entries erased sooner.
  • Technical logs: for the short periods Supabase and PowerSync keep their logs.
  • Emails: as described in section 6.
  • Backups: until the providers' backups expire; they are never used to restore deleted data.

10. Security

  • All data between the app and our servers travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Each account can read and change only its own data (row-level security).
  • Our providers encrypt stored data on their infrastructure.
  • On your phone, data sits in storage only the app can access, protected further by your device's encryption and screen lock.

No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we process to a minimum.

11. What Hercules doesn't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No profiling or automated decisions (Art. 22 GDPR). Training suggestions are calculated on your phone and are only suggestions.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy of it (Art. 15)
  • correct it, which you can mostly do in the app (Art. 16)
  • erase it: delete entries or your whole account in the app, or ask us (Art. 17)
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18)
  • receive your synced data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests (sections 5, 6 and 14) on grounds relating to your situation (Art. 21)
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the EU country where you live (Art. 77)

To use these rights, email herculesfitnessapp@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

13. Children

Hercules is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
